About Us

Fern and Petal Cafe started as a tiny corner stall with one coffee machine and a big dream. Over the years it grew into the cozy garden cafe you see today.

Every morning we bake our pastries fresh, grind our beans by hand, and fill the space with flowers from the local market. Our goal has always been simple: give every guest a warm, calm place to slow down and enjoy the little things.
